Reflection:

  1. The most significant issue was the banner image’s fixed size in the original stylesheet. A fixed image can overflow its container on a narrow screen or look stretched.
  2. I used box-sizing: border-box for all elements and set the image to max-width: 100% and height: auto. This lets the image shrink while keeping its proportions. I also added media queries to adjust the card and text sizes on smaller screens.
  3. At 1280 pixels, the card uses 80% of the available width. At 768 and 390 pixels, it becomes full width and the text gets smaller. The image can resize, and the navigation links remain stacked vertically.
  4. Comparing the layouts at different widths helps me check whether text wraps clearly, images stay within the page, and links remain easy to use. It also showed me that a layout that looks fine on a desktop may need changes for phones.
  5. Next, I would add a centered container with a maximum width, improve spacing between sections, and keep body text closer to 16 pixels on phones for easier reading.
